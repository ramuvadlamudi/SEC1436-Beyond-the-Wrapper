# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or role.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and security boundaries.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Prioritise the safety of users and affected communities.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised conduct, sustained disruption, deliberate exposure of private data, or publishing vulnerabilities irresponsibly are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour violates this code. Reports should use the private project-owner channel described in `SECURITY.md`; do not include sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and impartially as practical.
